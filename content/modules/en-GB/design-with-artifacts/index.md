# Design drafts with artifacts

!!! note "Synthetic content"
    This module is mock material for the platform proof of concept.

Ask for a rough layout, flow diagram or mock-up as an artifact, then iterate on it in the conversation.

- Describe the audience and the one job the design must do.
- Ask for two contrasting options, not one.
- Treat the output as a sketch for discussion, not a final asset.
