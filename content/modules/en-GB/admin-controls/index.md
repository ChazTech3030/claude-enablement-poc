This module is for workspace owners and administrators.

## The admin console

The admin console is where owners manage members, roles, billing and organisation-wide settings. Changes apply to everyone in the organisation, so agree them with your governance group first.

## Single sign-on and provisioning

Connecting your identity provider means people sign in with their work account, and leavers lose access when their account is disabled.

1. Verify your organisation's email domain.
2. Configure single sign-on with your identity provider.
3. Enable automatic provisioning so accounts follow your directory.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Change organisation settings and billing |
| Admin | Manage members |
| Member | Use Claude within the organisation's settings |
