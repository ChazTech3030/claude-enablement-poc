# Data handling basics

!!! note "Synthetic content"
    This module is mock material for the platform proof of concept.

Claude is most useful when it has context. This module sets out what context is appropriate to share, and how your organisation's settings affect what happens to it.

## Three categories

| Category | Examples | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Published reports, marketing copy | Share freely |
| Internal | Plans, internal process documents | Share within your approved workspace |
| Restricted | Personal data, credentials, regulated data | Follow your data protection lead's rules; never paste credentials |

## Retention

Your workspace owner sets how long conversations are kept. Shorter retention reduces exposure but removes history people may rely on. Agree the period with your data protection lead and record the decision.

## Projects and shared knowledge

Files added to a project are available to everyone in that project. Treat a project like a shared drive: check who is a member before adding documents.

## Quick rules

- Remove names and identifiers when an example works just as well without them.
- Never paste passwords, keys or tokens.
- If in doubt, ask before sharing.
