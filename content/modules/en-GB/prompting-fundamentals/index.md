# Prompting fundamentals

!!! note "Synthetic content"
    This module is mock material for the platform proof of concept.

Good prompts read like a good brief to a capable colleague who has never seen your work before.

## Be explicit about the task

State what you want, who it is for and what "good" looks like. "Summarise this" is weaker than "Summarise this report in five bullet points for a finance director who has not read it".

## Give context

Paste or attach the material Claude needs. Explain any internal terms. If there is a house style, say so or give an example.

## Show an example

One worked example of the output you want is often worth several sentences of description.

## Iterate

Treat the first answer as a draft. Say what to change and why ("shorter", "less formal", "add the risks section") rather than starting again.

## Practice

Rewrite this weak prompt into a strong one:

> Write an email about the project.
